About Me

A Legacy of Disciplined Investing

Building Wealth Through Discipline & Long-Term Vision

Born into a middle-class family in Singapore that placed a strong emphasis on education, I developed an early interest in capital flows, risk management, and macroeconomics during Singapore's emergence as an international financial hub in the early 1990s.

In 1989, I enrolled at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), majoring in Engineering and Applied Mathematics. During my university years, I demonstrated a strong interest in quantitative analysis, system modeling, and uncertainty analysis, which later became the foundation of my expertise in risk management and asset allocation.

After graduation, rather than pursuing an academic career, I chose to enter the financial industry directly to understand "real-world risk" from the market itself.
